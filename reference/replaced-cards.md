# Rootbound: Discovery cards replaced by the second edition

These 36 cards were taken out of the Forest, Ruins and Crystal Fields decks when the new OpenArt cards went in.
Format: **Title** (copies) | Subheading | Flavour | Card text.
Flavour lines marked * were drafted for the review list and never printed on a card.

## Forest (15 cards, 16 copies)

- **Supply Cache** (2) | Resource • Forest | Wrapped in leaves, tied with root-string, left for whoever needs it.* | Gain 2 Wood and 1 Food.
- **Discovery Trail** (1) | Resource • Forest | Small footprints, still fresh, lead somewhere worth going.* | Gain 1 Essence and 1 Wood.
- **Hidden Village** (1) | Resource • Forest | Doors no taller than a toadstool, and every one of them open.* | Gain 1 Wood, 1 Food and 1 Metal.
- **Essence Vein** (1) | Resource • Forest | The ground glows where the old magic runs close.* | Gain 2 Essence.
- **Mushroom Forge** (1) | Resource • Forest | Someone’s fire still burns inside the hollow stump.* | Gain 2 Metal and 1 Essence.
- **Forest Shrine** (1) | Resource • Forest | Leave an offering, or ask for a blessing.* | Choose one: gain 1 Essence, or gain 1 Reputation.
- **Wild Strawberry Patch** (1) | Resource • Forest | Sweet, and only a little bit poisonous. | Gain 3 Food.
- **Whispering Spirit** (1) | Ally • Forest | It speaks only in rustles, but it points the way.* | Gain 1 Essence. Draw another card.
- **Mushroom Scout** (1) | Ally • Forest | Been there already. Back already, too.* | Gain 1 Food. +2 movement this turn. (Still in the Swamp deck.)
- **Forest Stag** (1) | Ally • Forest | It walks where the brambles let no one else through.* | Gain 1 Wood and 1 Essence. Difficult terrain costs only 1 movement this turn.
- **Ancient Treant** (1) | Ally • Forest | It lifts you over the roots like a leaf.* | Gain 2 Wood. +1 movement this turn.
- **Owl of the Deep Wood** (1) | Ally • Forest | She has watched this forest for a thousand winters. | Reveal 1 unexplored tile. Gain 1 Reputation.
- **Night Journey** (1) | Event • Forest | The moon shows a path the sun never did.* | +2 movement this turn. Draw another card.
- **Luminous Fields** (1) | Event • Forest | The whole meadow lights up, and everyone feels it.* | Every player gains 1 Essence. (Also removed from Crystal Fields.)
- **Rootbound** (1) | Relic • Forest | The heart of the old forest, and it has chosen you.* | Gain 3 Essence, 2 Wood and 2 Food. Draw 2 more cards.

Kept in the Forest deck for now: Forest Wraith and Bramble Thicket.

## Ruins (11 cards, 13 copies)

- **Forgotten Workshop** (2) | Resource • Ruins | Benches, tools, and a half-built thing nobody finished.* | Gain 1 Metal and 1 Wood. (The new card of the same name gives 2 Metal.)
- **Ancient Ruin** (2) | Resource • Ruins | Old stones, older secrets.* | Gain 1 Metal and 1 Essence.
- **Ancient Monolith** (1) | Resource • Ruins | Its runes rearrange themselves when you look away.* | Gain 1 Essence and 1 Metal. Draw another card.
- **Gearsmith’s Toolbox** (1) | Resource • Ruins | Every tool still oiled, as if its owner just stepped out. | Gain 2 Metal and 1 Wood.
- **Stone Guardian** (1) | Ally • Ruins | None shall pass. But here, take this.* | Gain 1 Metal. Your movement ends. (Still in the Highlands deck.)
- **Ancient Relic** (1) | Relic • Ruins | Nobody remembers what it was for. Everybody wants it.* | Gain 1 Essence and 1 Metal. Gain 1 Reputation.
- **Lost Vault** (1) | Relic • Ruins | Behind the waterfall, a door that remembers. | Gain 1 Artifact. Gain 1 Reputation.
- **Clockwork Remnant** (1) | Relic • Ruins | Its gears still turn, very slowly. | Gain 1 Artifact and 1 Metal. Gain 1 Reputation.
- **Sunken Reliquary** (1) | Relic • Ruins | Some treasures are guarded. | Gain 1 Artifact. Gain 1 Reputation. A Corrupted Wolf appears on your tile.
- **Rust Mites** (1) | Enemy • Ruins | A crunching sound from inside your backpack. | Lose 2 Metal.
- **Collapsing Arch** (1) | Enemy • Ruins | The ruin finally gives way. | Lose 1 life.

- **Crystal Vein** (2) | Resource • Crystal Fields | A seam of light running through the rock.* | Gain 2 Crystal.

## Unchanged

The quest-mode Story cards (Rumours, Landmarks, the Broken Compass) are still shuffled into these decks when Quests are on.
