# Rootbound tutorial: narration script

Narrator: warm storyteller (ElevenLabs "George"). About 4.5 minutes.
Each scene lists what is on screen, then the words spoken.

## 1. Cold open (cinematic shots from the cover and card art)
Deep in the Everdeep, where blades of grass tower like trees and a single dewdrop can hold the whole sky, there lived a small and curious people: the Mushroomfolk.
For a thousand years they sheltered beneath the oldest tree in the forest. Then the Blight found its way into his heartwood.

## 2. Title
Rootbound.

## 3. The goal (slow flyover of the board)
Rootbound is a cooperative game for one to five players. You win or lose together.
Your party of Mushroomfolk inventors must explore the forest, gather what it offers, and build a marvellous invention at the Workshop, before the corruption spreading through the old tree's roots wakes the Root King.

## 4. The heroes (turntable of each figure)
Choose your Mushroomfolk. Mushy the Scout moves through rough ground with ease. Bramblecap the Trapper rolls an extra die in every fight. Morella the Dreamwalker sees two cards for every one. Sporewick the Alchemist can rest anywhere, and cleanses for free. And Thorncap the Guardian takes the blow so his friends don't have to.

## 5. The board (camera sweeps low across the tiles)
The forest is a ring of hex tiles around the Workshop at its heart. Most of it starts face down, hidden in mist. Forest, Ruins, Crystal Fields, Swamp and Highlands each have their own deck of discoveries, and their own resources: Food, Wood, Metal, Crystal and Essence.

## 6. Your turn (a figure sets out and hops tile to tile)
On your turn, first choose how to set out. Eat a Food for three moves, spend a life for three moves, or trudge just one step on an empty stomach.
Then you have two decisions to spend, before, after, or between your moves. Step onto an unexplored tile and it flips over, revealing what lies beneath.

## 7. Searching (a Discovery card lifts from its deck and turns over)
Search a tile to draw from its Discovery deck. You might find a hidden orchard, a helpful ally, an ancient relic... or something waiting in the dark.

## 8. Gathering and building (resources fly into a backpack; a Skyship part is built)
Gather to take the land's resources, but each tile only gives so much: gather it again and you risk disturbing it, and over-harvested land may turn to rot.
Carry what you find home to the Workshop, unpack it into storage, and build the parts of your invention, one by one.

## 9. Corruption and the Horror Track (tiles crack with glowing magenta veins; the track fills)
At the end of every round, the forest stirs. Corruption spreads from tile to tile, and every step it takes climbs the Horror Track. Every third round is Night, when the forest stirs even harder.
Every three steps of Horror, a creature of the Blight crawls out to hunt. Each round, every monster rolls the die three times and lurches that way, corrupting the ground where it stops.

## 10. Fighting (dice tumble across the table; a monster takes damage)
To fight, roll the risk dice: Hits and Double Hits deal damage. Every friend on or beside the monster adds a die, so fight together. But a monster that survives strikes back at everyone close by, unless it's caught in a snare.

## 11. Cleansing and Fear (a corrupted tile heals; Horror falls back)
Spend Essence to cleanse a corrupted tile. It earns you Reputation, and pushes the Horror Track back. Beware of Fear: shrug off two, but the third sends you into a panic.

## 12. The Root King (the camera rises to the Root King on the edge of the forest)
If the Horror Track reaches twenty, the Root King awakens and marches on the Workshop. Face him only when you are sure: an attack that doesn't bring him down costs your life. Gather your friends around him first, and strike as one.

## 13. Winning (the finished Skyship; the party gathers at the Workshop)
Build every part, stock enough Food for the voyage, gather everyone still standing at the Workshop, and launch. The forest is saved, for now.

## 14. Outro (wide shot of the whole board, then the logo)
Gather your friends, and set out together. Rootbound.
